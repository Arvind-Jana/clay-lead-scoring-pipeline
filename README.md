# 🎯 Intelligent B2B Account Scoring & Lead Qualification Pipeline

[![Tool: Clay](https://img.shields.io/badge/Platform-Clay-purple.svg)](https://clay.com)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

An automated sales intelligence and prospect qualification pipeline engineered in **Clay**, profiling and scoring 25 high-priority enterprise accounts using custom firmographic indicators, technology telemetry, and decision-maker signals.

---

## 🌟 Executive Summary
Modern sales and go-to-market teams waste up to 40% of their prospecting cycles on unverified leads and ill-fitting target accounts. This project establishes an automated account qualification engine that enriches, scores, and tiers target enterprise accounts based on rigorous Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) criteria.

---

## 🛠️ Pipeline Architecture & Methodology
1. **Target Account Extraction**: Curated 25 high-growth enterprise accounts across SaaS, fintech, and AI sectors.
2. **Automated Multi-Source Enrichment**: Integrated Clay data providers to pull verified employee headcount growth, tech stack telemetry, and executive leadership personas.
3. **Quantitative Scoring Algorithm**:
   - **Tier 1 (High Fit)**: Verified decision-makers + strong headcount growth + compatible technology stack.
   - **Tier 2 (Moderate Fit)**: Target sector with partial signals requiring secondary outreach testing.
   - **Tier 3 (Nurture)**: Qualified accounts lacking current buying signals.
4. **Data Validation & Deduplication**: Enforced schema checks to eliminate missing attributes, stale emails, and duplicate domains.
